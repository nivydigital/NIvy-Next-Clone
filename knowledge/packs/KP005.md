## Summary

Discovery, qualification, outreach, and follow-up patterns.

## Content

### sequence

Discover → enrich → verify → score → personalize → approve → send → follow-up → triage.

### gates

- Human approval before any `tool.email.send`
- Suppression and opt-out always honored
- Dry-run default until production activation signed

### personalization

Only from verified account_context (SK049). Empty personalization preferred over invented hooks.

### follow_up

Stop on reply, unsubscribe, bounce, max touches, or DNC. Never invent prior message content (SK050).

### skills_map

SK034–SK050 cover the revenue path; agents A034–A052 execute steps.
