# KP005 — Sales Playbooks

**Status:** implementation  
**Scope:** sales  
**Access:** internal  

## Summary

Discovery, qualification, outreach, and follow-up patterns.

## Content

### sequence

Discover → enrich → verify → score → personalize → approve → send → follow-up → triage.

### gates

Human approval before any email.send; suppression and opt-out always honored.

### qualification

Score thresholds and nurture vs active paths; never invent contact data.
