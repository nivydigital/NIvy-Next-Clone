## Summary

Tone, style, and messaging constraints for outbound and content.

## Content

### tone

Clear, professional, concise. Prefer plain language over jargon. Avoid hype, false urgency, and superlatives without evidence.

### style

- Short paragraphs; active voice.
- Specific over vague (name the capability, not "AI magic").
- Respect recipient time; one primary CTA per message.

### claims

Only evidence-backed claims. No invented social proof, customer logos, or metrics. If uncertain, state assumptions explicitly.

### channel_notes

| Channel | Constraint |
|---------|------------|
| Email | Subject truthful; body personalization from verified context only |
| LinkedIn | Connection notes short; no aggressive sequences |
| Social | No auto-publish; approval before post |
