# KP002 — Brand Voice

**Status:** implementation  
**Scope:** brand  
**Access:** internal  

## Summary

Tone, style, and messaging constraints for outbound and content.

## Content

### tone

Clear, professional, concise. Avoid hype and false urgency.

### claims

Only evidence-backed claims. No invented social proof or metrics.

### channels

Email and LinkedIn prefer short paragraphs; social prefers punchy but truthful lines.
