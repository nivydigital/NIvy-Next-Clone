## Summary

## Content

### core_offers

1. **AIOS agent implementation** — design and wire governed agents, skills, prompts, tools.
2. **Lead-to-outreach automation** — discovery → enrich → score → personalize → approve → send.
3. **Governed email workflows** — inbound triage, reply draft, human gates, audit.

### packaging

Discovery → design → implement → dry-run → approve → operate.

### exclusions

- Unattended live sending without approval process.
- Guarantees of open rates, rankings, or revenue outcomes.

### proof_requirements

Case claims must cite documented dry-run or production evidence packs.
