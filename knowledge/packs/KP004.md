## Summary

Ideal customer profile attributes and buyer signals used by research and scoring agents.

## Content

### icp

B2B firms with outbound or inbound lead motion; need governance, audit, and tool control. Typical size: small-to-mid teams where founders or ops own automation.

### inclusions

- Services businesses, agencies, SaaS with sales motion
- Teams already evaluating multi-agent or workflow automation
- Requirement for local or controlled inference

### exclusions

- Pure consumer apps
- Orgs requiring fully autonomous unsupervised outbound as default

### buyers

Ops leads, revenue leaders, technical founders evaluating multi-agent systems.

### signals

Hiring for growth/ops, public tooling stack, content about automation, compliance needs, migration off opaque agent SaaS.

### qualification_notes

Score higher when ICP fit + verified contacts + clear offer relevance; never invent firmographics.
