## Summary

Core company identity, mission, markets served, and operating principles for Nivy Digital.

## Content

### identity

Nivy Digital builds governed multi-agent business automation (AIOS) for sales, marketing, and ops. The product emphasizes fail-closed execution, audit trails, and human approval before external side effects.

### markets

Primary focus: B2B services companies needing lead-to-revenue automation with human approval gates. Secondary: operators who need local inference (Ollama) and explicit tool control rather than opaque SaaS agents.

### principles

1. Fail closed on missing inputs or policy violations.
2. No invented facts, contacts, or metrics.
3. Side effects only via approved tools after authorization.
4. Provenance required for material claims.
5. Prefer dry-run until production activation checklist is signed.

### non_goals

- Autonomous production email without approval records.
- Bulk loading untrusted documents into RAG.
